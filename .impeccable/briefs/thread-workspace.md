# Thread workspace

Mode: Operate. Existing Inter, mint/slate palette, cube mark and light/dark themes remain authoritative. The Owner reads and replies to one Thread, opens artifacts, and moves between independent conversations without losing drafts or attachments.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A narrow global icon rail gives the conversation its space. A focused Thread becomes an inset work surface; Home remains an open, titleless canvas.

OWN-WORLD: Incumbent Inter, quiet mint selection, slate content, fine borders and the original cube. Consistent licensed Lucide line icons carry utilities, with accessible names, tooltips and 44px touch targets.

STORY: The Owner sees whether they are at Home or inside a Thread through the selected rail icon, frame and back affordance. The branch icon opens the Thread drawer; selection closes it and restores the conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 56px rail contains Home, Threads, New thread, global artifacts and activity, with Settings at the bottom. Home has no heading or duplicated utility bar. A focused Thread occupies the remaining inset framed surface, with a compact back/name/Conversation/Artifacts/actions strip. Its composer stays at the bottom; detailed execution remains disclosed. Phone keeps the rail and a tighter frame, with a modal drawer and full-screen artifact preview.

FORM: Approved option A, icon rail and focus frame, direction key compact-thread-context. User approval: “lets go with A”. The two panels in the proposal describe states of ONE app. GLOBAL/IN A THREAD are comparison-only labels. Duplicate global top utilities are omitted as approved.

Signature interaction: the selected branch icon connects visually to the focused frame; Home removes the frame and context strip. Thread rows show authoritative working duration, queue count and last turn outcome separately from attention, unread and explicit settlement. Row, context-strip and palette actions share one definition. The drawer preserves canvas colors with a transparent backdrop and retains native modal focus. Drawers and previews return keyboard focus. Motion remains short control feedback, respecting reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
